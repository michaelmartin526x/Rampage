Rules & paytable
Demo credits only. Winning symbols disappear, survivors drop down and new symbols refill from the top. Every cascade can pay again; the spin total includes all wins. Scatters remain and four or more unlock the bonus after the chain ends. Four horizontal lines and two diagonals. Three matches pay from either edge; four matching symbols (including wilds) pay once only, with no reverse three-symbol award. Wild substitutes for all except Scatter. Four scatters start two rooms. Each room contains 2–5 mystery prizes: whole-pound cash and optional ×2, ×3, ×5 or ×50 tokens. Add all multiplier tokens, then multiply the room’s combined cash once; without a multiplier, cash pays as shown. Feature awards: 50× (56%), 100× (34%), 200× (10%); average 82×. The two room awards share the selected feature total. Test Bonus does not change balance.
